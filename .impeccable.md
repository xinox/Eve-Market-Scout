# Design Context

## Product

EVE Market Scout is a decision tool for station traders. It turns current EVE Online market snapshots into fast comparisons: where an item is cheap, where demand exists, and whether a route still has margin after fees.

## Audience

Experienced and aspiring capsuleer traders who scan dense data repeatedly. They value speed, trustworthy numbers, legible tables, and honest empty/error states over spectacle.

## Brand personality

Calm, industrial, exact.

## Aesthetic direction

A restrained market terminal: warm paper and graphite neutrals, ink-blue structure, and rare mineral-green or warning-amber accents. Archivo gives headings a broad technical presence; Source Sans 3 keeps dense data readable. Dark and light themes are equally intentional. Avoid neon cyan/purple, glowing cyberpunk, arcade motifs, gradients, excessive cards, and decorative HUD clutter.

## Design principles

1. Put the next trading decision above decoration.
2. Tables are the primary interface and must remain usable on narrow screens.
3. Use tabular numerals, explicit units, and visible data timestamps.
4. Use color only for status, profit/loss, selection, and warnings.
5. Keep controls compact but never cryptic; preserve clear focus and hover states.
6. Label demo, stale, missing, and live data unambiguously.
